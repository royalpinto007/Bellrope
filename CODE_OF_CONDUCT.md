# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Being welcoming and respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Showing empathy towards other contributors

Things that do not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit
or reject contributions that do not follow them, or ban temporarily or
permanently any contributor whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private report with the repository
owner. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
